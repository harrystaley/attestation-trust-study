## Your rights and privacy
Your participation was voluntary and your responses are anonymous and confidential.
Your data will be stored in a secure database and will not be shared with any third parties..

The follwing safeguards have been put in place:
- Anonymous data collection: no name collected, IP addresses are stripped on submission. This is your core protection.
- IP address stripping: your report's bot/fraud section says, "IP addresses are stripped on submission, so duplicate screening relies on the platform mechanism rather than stored addresses."
- Age as ranges, not exact: you deliberately collect age as buckets (18–24, etc.) rather than birthdate/exact age, which reduces re-identification risk. That's a real, considered protection.
