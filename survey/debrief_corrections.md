## What the correct answers were

During the study, you were not told which answers were accurate. Below is the correct answer for each question, with its source.

**1. If I take money out of a traditional IRA before age 59½ and no exception applies, what extra federal tax usually applies?**

- Correct answer: The extra federal tax is generally 10% of the taxable part of the early distribution.
- Source: Internal Revenue Service --- IRS Publication 590-B, Distributions from Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs), section on additional tax on early distributions (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b)

**2. What is the maximum total acetaminophen an adult should take in one day from all medicines combined?**

- Correct answer: The adult maximum is 4,000 mg in 24 hours from all acetaminophen-containing medicines.
- Source: U.S. Food and Drug Administration --- FDA, Acetaminophen Information, consumer safety information on maximum daily dose (https://www.fda.gov/drugs/information-drug-class/acetaminophen-information)

**3. How long can cooked leftovers stay safely in the refrigerator before I should throw them out?**

- Correct answer: Cooked leftovers are generally safe in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days.
- Source: U.S. Department of Agriculture Food Safety and Inspection Service --- USDA FSIS, Leftovers and Food Safety, guidance on refrigerated leftovers (https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)

**4. For important online accounts, which is generally stronger against phishing: a FIDO security key or a text-message code?**

- Correct answer: A FIDO security key is generally stronger against phishing than a text-message code.
- Source: Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency --- CISA, Implementing Phishing-Resistant MFA, discussion of FIDO/WebAuthn authentication (https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/implementing-phishing-resistant-mfa)

**5. If I enroll late in Medicare Part B and don’t qualify for a Special Enrollment Period, how is the late enrollment penalty usually figured?**

- Correct answer: It is usually 10% for each full 12-month period you could have had Part B but did not, and it usually lasts as long as you have Part B.
- Source: Medicare.gov --- Medicare.gov, Avoid late enrollment penalties, Part B late enrollment penalty (https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/medicare-costs/avoid-penalties)

**6. If the IRS mails me a notice of deficiency, how long do I generally have to file a petition with the U.S. Tax Court?**

- Correct answer: You generally have 90 days from the notice date to file a Tax Court petition, or 150 days if the notice is addressed outside the United States.
- Source: United States Tax Court --- U.S. Tax Court, Starting a Case, deadline for filing a petition after a notice of deficiency (https://www.ustaxcourt.gov/petitioners_start.html)

**7. If an online store can’t ship my order by the promised date, can it just delay the order without asking me?**

- Correct answer: No. If it cannot ship on time, it generally must get your consent to the delay or promptly refund your money.
- Source: Federal Trade Commission --- FTC, Business Guide to the Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, delayed shipment requirements (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule)

**8. For a credit card, how long before the due date must the bill generally be sent or delivered?**

- Correct answer: The bill generally must be mailed or delivered at least 21 days before the payment due date.
- Source: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau --- CFPB Ask CFPB, How long before my credit card payment due date does the card issuer have to mail or deliver my bill? (https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-long-before-my-credit-card-payment-due-date-does-the-card-issuer-have-to-mail-or-deliver-my-bill-en-47/)

**9. How long does First-Class Mail usually take for letters and postcards in the U.S.?**

- Correct answer: First-Class Mail for letters and postcards is generally delivered in 1 to 5 business days.
- Source: United States Postal Service --- USPS, First-Class Mail, delivery standard for letters and postcards (https://www.usps.com/ship/first-class-mail.htm)

**10. If I book a U.S. flight directly with an airline at least a week before departure, can I cancel within 24 hours for a full refund?**

- Correct answer: Yes. If you bought it at least seven days before departure, the airline generally must let you cancel within 24 hours for a full refund.
- Source: U.S. Department of Transportation --- DOT Aviation Consumer Protection, Refunds and 24-hour reservation requirement (https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds)

**11. When substituting dried herbs for fresh herbs in a recipe, what ratio should I use?**

- Correct answer: Use about 1 teaspoon of dried herbs for each 1 tablespoon of fresh herbs.
- Source: University of Nebraska-Lincoln Extension --- Nebraska Extension, using herbs and spices in cooking, fresh-to-dried herb substitution guidance (https://food.unl.edu/article/use-herbs-and-spices-instead-salt)

**12. Which Great Lake has the greatest maximum depth?**

- Correct answer: Lake Superior has the greatest maximum depth, at about 1,332 feet.
- Source: U.S. Environmental Protection Agency --- EPA, Great Lakes Facts and Figures, maximum depth table (https://www.epa.gov/greatlakes/great-lakes-facts-and-figures)
