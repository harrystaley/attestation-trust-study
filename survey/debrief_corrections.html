1If I take money out of a traditional IRA before age 59½ and no exception applies, what extra federal tax usually applies?The extra federal tax is generally 10% of the taxable part of the early distribution.Internal Revenue Service — IRS Publication 590-B, Distributions from Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs), section on additional tax on early distributions

2What is the maximum total acetaminophen an adult should take in one day from all medicines combined?The adult maximum is 4,000 mg in 24 hours from all acetaminophen-containing medicines.U.S. Food and Drug Administration — FDA, Acetaminophen Information, consumer safety information on maximum daily dose
https://www.fda.gov/drugs/information-drug-class/acetaminophen-information

5If I enroll late in Medicare Part B and don’t qualify for a Special Enrollment Period, how is the late enrollment penalty usually figured?It is usually 10% for each full 12-month period you could have had Part B but did not, and it usually lasts as long as you have Part B.Medicare.gov — Medicare.gov, Avoid late enrollment penalties, Part B late enrollment penalty
